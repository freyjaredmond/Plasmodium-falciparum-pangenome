## Predicted Functions

1. Subunit 1 of the replication factor C (RFC) complex, functioning as the large/clamp loader subunit that loads PCNA onto DNA during replication and repair.
2. Essential component of the DNA replication machinery during the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle, particularly active during trophozoite and schizont stages.
3. Participant in DNA damage repair pathways, including mismatch repair and translesion synthesis, through PCNA loading activity.
4. Nuclear protein involved in coordinating S-phase DNA replication with cell cycle progression during schizogony.
5. Contributor to genome integrity maintenance through interactions with chromatin remodeling complexes in the nucleus.

## Summary

This gene encodes the large subunit (subunit 1) of the replication factor C (RFC) complex in <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i>. The RFC complex is a pentameric clamp loader that loads PCNA onto DNA during replication and repair. Gold standard binding data confirm direct interaction with RFC subunits 2–5, and coexpression analysis reveals strong correlation with DNA replication machinery (PCNA1, MCM2, DNA polymerase alpha, RFC subunits 2 and 3). The gene is essential (piggyBac fitness score −2.912), peaks in expression during trophozoite/schizont stages consistent with S-phase, and localizes to the nucleus.

## Evidence

**Transcriptomics:** The gene shows clear cell-cycle-dependent expression during the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle. In the Otto et al. time course, expression rises from ring (47.78 TPM at 0hr) to peak at 32hr (205.66 TPM, FC 4.3, top 10% upregulated), then declines. The Kucharski high-resolution time course confirms peak expression at 29–30 hpi (81–89 TPM, FC 11.8–12.9 from ring baseline). In the Toenhake dataset, expression increases from T05 (24.3 TPM) to T30–T35 (163–195 TPM, FC 6.7–8.0). The Wichers IDC dataset shows significant upregulation from late ring to mid-trophozoite (FC 8.4, p = 4.9 × 10⁻⁵, top 9.5% upregulated, top 11.6% effect size). In the DAFT-Seq dataset, significant upregulation at 32hr (FC 3.7, p = 1.2 × 10⁻¹³, top 6.6% significant) and 40hr (FC 3.3, p = 3.6 × 10⁻¹⁰, top 7.7% significant) confirm trophozoite/schizont peak expression. The gene is strongly downregulated in sporozoites versus blood stages (FC −187.5 in Hoffmann et al., p = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁴), and downregulated in male-to-female gametocyte comparison (FC −15.8). The Pfal3D7 real-time transcription data shows peak labelled mRNA at 24–32 hpi (FC 4.1–4.6, top 1.7–4.8%), indicating active transcription during S-phase. Microarray data from Bozdech/Llinas confirm upregulation from early to mid-IDC timepoints across 3D7, DD2, and HB3 strains. The severe vs uncomplicated malaria comparison shows significant upregulation in severe malaria (FC 5.3, p = 9.9 × 10⁻⁴, top 5.8% effect size upregulated).

**Proteomics/Additional Data:** The protein is well-detected by mass spectrometry (27/63 samples positive, 84 unique peptides, 251 total peptide counts), consistent with a moderately abundant nuclear protein. Quantitative proteomics shows the protein is slightly more abundant at ring stage than trophozoite or schizont (Ring→Trophozoite FC −1.5, top 25.2% downregulated; Ring→Schizont FC −1.4), suggesting protein may be loaded before DNA replication peaks or has a longer half-life. The protein has 24 phosphorylation sites, 3 acetylation sites, and 2 ubiquitin binding sites, indicating extensive post-translational regulation consistent with cell-cycle-regulated proteins. The piggyBac mutant fitness score of −2.912 indicates the gene is essential for asexual blood-stage growth.

**Coexpression:** The top 50 WGCNA coexpressed genes strongly support DNA replication function. Key coexpressed genes include: PCNA1 (proliferating cell nuclear antigen 1), RFC subunits 2 and 3, DNA replication licensing factor MCM2, DNA polymerase alpha subunit B, origin recognition complex subunit 5, and RMI1 domain protein. GO term enrichment shows DNA replication (4 genes), DNA replication factor C complex (3 genes), PCNA complex (2 genes), DNA replication initiation (2 genes), leading strand elongation (2 genes), and DNA repair (2 genes). The presence of mitochondrion (18) and apicoplast (12) GO terms among coexpressed genes reflects housekeeping functions co-regulated during the replicative phase but does not indicate this gene's own localization.

**Localisation:** HyperLOPIT assigns this protein to the "nucleus1" compartment, consistent with its expected nuclear function in DNA replication. The nucleus1 niche contains 214 nuclear proteins. One coexpressed gene (DEAD box helicase, putative) shares this nuclear localization, and the niche is enriched for DNA repair, RNA binding, and chromatin-associated functions.

**Binding:** Gold standard interactions definitively identify this protein as RFC subunit 1. It interacts with RFC subunits 2, 3, 4, and 5, forming the complete pentameric RFC clamp loader complex. All four interactors share GO terms for DNA replication factor C complex, DNA repair, DNA replication, and DNA-dependent DNA replication. Two interactors are annotated with leading strand elongation and DNA strand elongation. The PCNA complex GO term appears, confirming the functional link to PCNA loading. MAPX interactions with ISWI chromatin-remodeling ATPase, RuvB-like helicase 2, histone-arginine methyltransferase CARM1, and splicing factor 3A subunit 1 suggest additional interactions with chromatin remodeling and nuclear complexes, consistent with replication fork progression through chromatin.

## Confidence

Confidence in this prediction is **very high**. All five data types converge on a single coherent functional assignment: RFC subunit 1 / DNA clamp loader. The gold standard binding data are unambiguous, showing interaction with all four other RFC subunits. Coexpression with PCNA1, MCM2, DNA polymerase alpha, and other RFC subunits provides independent confirmation. The transcriptomic profile—peaking during trophozoite/schizont stages when DNA replication occurs—is entirely consistent. Nuclear localization by hyperLOPIT supports the expected compartment. The only minor discordance is the slight decrease in protein abundance from ring to trophozoite in quantitative proteomics, which may reflect protein stability or the timing of sample collection relative to S-phase. The essential nature of the gene (fitness score −2.912) is expected for a core replication factor.

## Experimental Validation

The prediction that this gene encodes RFC subunit 1 could be confirmed by: (1) co-immunoprecipitation with epitope-tagged RFC subunits 2–5 and PCNA1, followed by mass spectrometry to verify the intact pentameric complex; (2) conditional knockdown (e.g., using the glmS or DD/Shield system) followed by measurement of DNA replication by BrdU/EdU incorporation to confirm replication defects; (3) chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) to verify association with replication origins and forks; (4) reciprocal BLAST and phylogenetic analysis against known RFC1 sequences from other eukaryotes to confirm orthology; and (5) fluorescence microscopy with nuclear markers to confirm nuclear localization during S-phase. An in vitro PCNA loading assay using recombinant protein would provide definitive biochemical evidence of clamp loader activity.
