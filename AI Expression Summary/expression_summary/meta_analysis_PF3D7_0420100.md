**Ring-Stage-Predominant Expression with Progressive Decline Through the Intraerythrocytic Cycle**

## Summary

This <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i> gene is predominantly expressed in ring and early trophozoite stages, with consistent transcriptional decline through the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (IDC) across multiple independent experiments and strains.

- **Peak expression occurs in early rings** (74–238 TPM at 0–4 hr), declining 3- to 6-fold by late schizont stages (FC −5.1 at 42 hr, top 15.6% downregulated; FC −6.0 at 40 hr in strand-specific data), with significant ring-to-schizont downregulation confirmed statistically (FC −2.6, p = 1.9e-9).
- **Strain-dependent variation** exists: HB3 expresses this gene significantly higher than 3D7 (FC 1.6, p = 2.2e-11).
- **Merozoite upregulation** relative to late schizonts (FC 1.8, top 13.6%) suggests a potential role in invasion or early ring establishment.
- **Transient upregulation during early gametocyte commitment** (FC 1.3–1.9, days 2–4 post-induction) followed by decline during maturation.
- **Late-stage transcriptional decline is driven by reduced transcription** rather than mRNA destabilisation, as stable mRNA pools remain constant while newly synthesised mRNA decreases.

The ring-stage predominance and merozoite enrichment suggest a potential role in post-invasion erythrocyte remodelling or early parasite establishment.

## Key Experiments

### Intraerythrocytic Developmental Cycle Expression

This gene shows robust ring/early trophozoite peak expression with progressive decline through the IDC, confirmed across multiple strains, technologies, and experimental designs.

- **Blood stage transcriptome (3D7) (Otto et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene is progressively downregulated across the intraerythrocytic cycle from 0hr rings (126 TPM) to late stages, reaching its strongest decline at 48hr (FC -3.6, top 23.7% downregulated) with a modest rebound at 8hr (FC 1.2), consistent with ring-stage-predominant expression.
- **High resolution intraerythrocytic time course transcriptome by RNA-Seq (Kucharski and Tripathi et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 5 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene peaks in early rings (74 TPM at 4hr) and is progressively downregulated through the IDC, reaching its nadir at 42hr (FC -5.1, top 15.6% downregulated) and 46hr (FC -4.0, top 10.8%), with the strongest percentile ranking at 50hr (FC -3.0, top 6.8% downregulated).
- **Erythrocytic expression time series (3D7, DD2, HB3) (Bozdech et al. and Llinas et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 5 | Confidence: N/A
  - Across all three strains (3D7, DD2, HB3), this gene shows consistent mild downregulation in later IDC stages relative to early rings, with the most notable changes in HB3 (FC -2.1, top 23.8% downregulated at 31-39hr) and a striking top 0.04% upregulation in DD2 at 9-16hr versus 1-8hr.
- **IDC in constant temperature and darkness (2020) (Subudhi et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - Under constant conditions, sense-strand expression declines from 238 TPM at 0hr to ~60 TPM by 40–48hr (FC -3.8 to -3.9, top ~48–51% downregulated), while antisense transcripts show a transient peak at 16hr (FC 3.7, top 28.9% upregulated) before declining, consistent with circadian-independent IDC stage regulation.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2018) (Toenhake et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: N/A
  - Sense-strand expression peaks at T10–T15 (~193–221 TPM, FC 2.0–2.3 vs T05) before declining, with antisense showing uniformly low expression and modest upregulation across timepoints; the overall expression pattern suggests early-to-mid trophozoite peak expression.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2019) (Wichers et al. 2019) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: 2
  - Expression is relatively stable through early-to-mid IDC stages (~125–205 TPM) with modest decline from mid-trophozoite to late trophozoite (p=0.004, FC -1.2) and ring to trophozoite transition showing significant downregulation (p=0.015).
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by DAFT-Seq (3D7, HB3, IT, 2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: 5
  - This gene is significantly more highly expressed in HB3 than 3D7 (FC 1.6, p=2.2e-11, top 37.9% upregulated) and modestly higher in IT (FC 1.1, p=1.4e-4); across the IDC in all strains, expression declines at 40hr (FC -1.4, p=0.003) with antisense showing stronger downregulation at 40–48hr.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by UTR-Seq (2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: 5
  - This gene peaks at 16hr (FC 1.7 vs 0hr, p=1.9e-13, top 39.7% upregulated) and remains significantly elevated through 40hr (p=2.2e-6), with high statistical confidence indicating consistent upregulation in trophozoite stages relative to rings.
- **Strand specific transcriptome of the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (Siegel et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene declines steadily from 69 TPM at 10hr to 11.5 TPM at 40hr (FC -6.0, top 34.7% downregulated), with the most pronounced drop in the schizont stage, consistent with ring/early trophozoite-predominant expression.
- **Transcriptome of the asexual life stages (Tang et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: 5
  - This gene is significantly downregulated from ring to schizont (FC -2.6, p=1.9e-9, top 52.6% downregulated) and ring to trophozoite (FC -1.3, p=2.6e-4), confirming ring-stage-predominant expression.
- **Pfal3D7 real-time transcription and decay ()** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - Labelled (newly transcribed) mRNA shows progressive decline from 0hpi through the IDC, reaching ~2-fold downregulation by 44–47hpi, while unlabelled (stable) mRNA remains remarkably constant throughout, suggesting transcriptional shutdown rather than mRNA destabilisation drives late-stage expression decline.

### Life Cycle and Sporozoite Expression

This gene shows modest upregulation in salivary gland sporozoites relative to oocysts and blood stages, with low overall expression in mosquito stages.

- **Asexual blood stages, salivary gland sporozoite and midgut oocyst transcriptomes (Gomez-Diaz) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 5 | Confidence: 2
  - This gene shows modest upregulation in salivary gland sporozoites versus asexual blood stages (FC 1.0, top 20.8% upregulated, p=0.004) with low expression across all stages; antisense transcripts show notable upregulation in oocysts and sporozoites versus blood stages (top 1.6–4.9% upregulated).
- **Life cycle expression data (3D7) (Le Roch et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene is moderately expressed throughout the erythrocytic cycle with the most notable change being upregulation in merozoites versus late schizonts (FC 1.8, top 13.6% upregulated) and progressive downregulation from early trophozoite to early schizogeny.
- **Oocyst and salivary gland sporozoite transcriptome comparison in <i>P. falciparum</i> (Lindner et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: 5
  - This gene is substantially upregulated in salivary gland sporozoites versus oocysts (FC 1.8, top 23.6% upregulated, p=1.5e-12), suggesting increased expression accompanies the transition to infectiousness.
- **Transcriptomes of 7 sexual and asexual life stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene peaks in early trophozoites (195 TPM) and declines through schizont stage, with subsequent upregulation in gametocyte II (58 TPM) and further increase in ookinete (122 TPM, FC 2.7 vs gametocyte V).
- **Strand specific transcriptomes of 4 life cycle stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene is strongly downregulated from schizont to gametocyte II (FC -10.2, top 28.9% downregulated) and upregulated in gametocyte V versus gametocyte II (sense FC 1.7, top 17.3%).
- **Comparison of in vitro versus aseptic mosquito produced sporozoites by RNA-seq (Eappen et al. 2022) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - Antisense transcripts are substantially upregulated in mosquito-produced sporozoites versus in vitro (FC 12.8, top 25.1% upregulated), while sense strand expression is near-zero in both conditions.

### Sexual Stage and Gametocyte Expression

Expression is transiently upregulated during early gametocyte commitment before declining through maturation.

- **Gametocyte time course from commitment to maturity (van Biljon et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene, lowly expressed pre-induction, shows transient upregulation during early gametocyte commitment (day 2 and 4 post-induction, FC 1.3–1.9, top 17–30% upregulated) before returning to baseline or slight downregulation by day 8–13.
- **Gametocyte stages I-V transcriptomes (Young et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene is moderately expressed at day 1 and shows consistent mild downregulation through gametocyte maturation, with the strongest decline by day 12 (FC -1.6, top 26.4% downregulated).
- **Gametocyte Transcriptomes (Lasonder et al.) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 3 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene shows minimal differential expression between male and female gametocytes at the sense level (FC -1.2, top 87.9% downregulated), though antisense transcripts are modestly upregulated in males versus females.
- **Sexually vs asexually committed schizont transcriptional profiles (Pelle et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 2 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene is moderately expressed in schizonts with slight downregulation in sexually committed versus asexually committed schizonts (FC -1.2, top 37.8% downregulated).

### Epigenetic and Transcriptional Regulators

Perturbation of chromatin-modifying enzymes produces modest but notable changes in this gene's expression.

- **Transcriptome of Bromodomain protein conditional knockouts over the IDC (Josling et al. 2015) (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - PfBDP1 knockdown results in notable upregulation of this gene (FC 1.2, top 11.4% upregulated), suggesting PfBDP1 may act as a repressor or that its depletion indirectly enhances this gene's expression.
- **Two Sir2 KO lines expression profiling (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - Sir2a and Sir2b knockouts show small but consistently notable changes versus wild-type, particularly Sir2a KO at ring stage (FC -1.1, top 11.4% downregulated) and Sir2b KO at schizont stage (FC -1.0, top 14.6% downregulated).

### Clinical and Field Isolate Expression

This gene shows modest expression variation across clinical phenotypes with some antisense regulation in binding phenotype comparisons.

- **Dual transcriptomes of malaria-infected Gambian children (RNA-Seq)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: 1
  - This gene shows no significant differential expression across clinical malaria phenotypes, with all p-values >0.01 except female vs male sense strand (p=0.015, FC -0.52); antisense transcripts show modest downregulation in hyperlactatemia vs uncomplicated (top 11.1% downregulated).
- **Transcriptome of sequestration phenotypes (Kamaliddin et al) (RNA-seq)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene shows modest expression differences between binding phenotypes, with antisense transcripts notably upregulated in ICAM1-binding versus HBEC-5i-binding parasites (top 18.4% upregulated).

### Drug Response

Chloroquine treatment produces genotype-dependent expression changes suggesting potential links to PfCRT-mediated resistance.

- **Three Isogenic Lines w/ CQ Treatment: expression profiles (Jiang et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - Chloroquine treatment induces notable upregulation in the 106-1 76I_352K line (FC 1.2, top 10.3% upregulated) and downregulation in the 106-1 parent line (FC -1.1, top 12.2% downregulated), suggesting PfCRT-genotype-dependent drug response.

### Invasion Pathway Knockouts

Minimal expression changes observed across invasion pathway mutants.

- **Invasion pathway knockouts (Stubbs et al.) (array)** | Biological Importance: 4 | Confidence: N/A
  - This gene shows minimal expression changes across invasion pathway knockout mutants (EBA140, EBA175, PfRh2b, SIR2) in 3D7, with the most notable changes being modest upregulation in W2mef EBA175 KO (FC 1.2, top 28.3% upregulated).
