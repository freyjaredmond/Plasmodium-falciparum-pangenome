**Peak Trophozoite-Stage Expression with Stage-Specific Regulation Across the <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i> Life Cycle**

This gene exhibits strong mid-cycle induction during the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (IDC), with peak expression at the trophozoite-to-schizont transition, and is preferentially expressed in blood stages over mosquito stages.

- **Trophozoite peak expression** is robustly supported: upregulation from ring to trophozoite reaches FC 12.6 (top 7.2%, p=7.2e-8) and from late ring to mid-trophozoite FC 12.7 (top 2.3%, p=2.7e-6), declining sharply at merozoite release.
- **Blood-stage enrichment**: rings are significantly upregulated relative to oocysts (FC 9.5, top 9.1%, p=2.1e-15), while sporozoites show significant downregulation versus blood stages (FC -26.6, p=0.044).
- **Gametocyte expression** is moderate, with early gametocyte upregulation (FC 3.6, day 6) declining during maturation (gametocyte II to V: FC -4.8, top 6.7% downregulated).
- **Translational regulation** is suggested by striking polysomal enrichment at ring stage (FC 55.5, top 4.4%, p=0.0036).
- **Strain variation** exists, with IT parasites showing significantly lower expression than 3D7 (FC -3.3, p=3.5e-4).

These patterns suggest a role in active intraerythrocytic growth or remodelling processes.

## Key Experiments

### Intraerythrocytic Developmental Cycle Expression

This gene consistently peaks at the trophozoite/schizont stage across multiple IDC time-course datasets, with significant upregulation from ring to trophozoite (FC 12.7, p=2.7e-6) and dramatic fold changes from low ring-stage baselines, declining at schizont/merozoite stages.

### Strain-Specific and Multi-Strain Comparisons

Significant inter-strain variation is observed, with IT parasites expressing this gene at significantly lower levels than 3D7 (FC -3.3, p=3.5e-4) and HB3 (FC -2.4, p=0.006), suggesting regulatory or polymorphic differences at this locus.

- Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by DAFT-Seq (3D7, HB3, IT, 2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq) | BI: 4 | C: 3
  - This gene peaks at 32 hr across strains (FC 2.6, top 25.3%, p=0.018) and shows significant inter-strain variation—notably lower in IT versus 3D7 (sense: FC -3.3, top 14.1%, p=3.5e-4).

### Life Cycle and Mosquito-Stage Expression

This gene is preferentially expressed in blood stages, with significant upregulation in rings versus oocysts (FC 9.5, p=2.1e-15) and significant downregulation in salivary gland sporozoites relative to oocysts (FC -2.9, p=2.4e-5).

- Ring, Oocyst and Sporozoite Transcriptomes (Zanghi et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - This gene is dramatically upregulated in rings relative to oocysts (sense: FC 9.5, top 9.1%, p=2.1e-15) and significantly downregulated in oocyst antisense relative to sporozoite antisense (FC -22.8, top 7.1–8.2%).
- Asexual blood stages, salivary gland sporozoite and midgut oocyst transcriptomes (Gomez-Diaz) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: 2
  - This gene shows modest upregulation in midgut oocysts versus asexual blood stages (antisense: top 1.6% upregulated) and is downregulated in salivary gland sporozoites relative to oocysts (sense: FC -2.0, p=0.0017), with low baseline expression across all stages.
- Comparison of in vitro versus aseptic mosquito produced sporozoites by RNA-seq (Eappen et al. 2022) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene is upregulated in mosquito-produced sporozoites compared to in vitro sporozoites in sense transcripts (FC 9.5, top 9.1% upregulated).

### Sexual Stage and Gametocyte Expression

This gene is expressed during gametocytogenesis with early-stage upregulation (peak at day 6, FC 3.6) declining during maturation, and shows male-biased expression in mature gametocytes. A sharp decline occurs from gametocyte II to V (FC -4.8, top 6.7% downregulated).

- Gametocyte time course from commitment to maturity (van Biljon et al.) (array) | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene is markedly upregulated during early-to-mid gametocytogenesis, peaking at day 6 post-induction (FC 3.6, top 11.6%) and day 4 (FC 2.9, top 11.1%), before declining at later stages.

- Gametocyte stages I-V transcriptomes (Young et al.) (array) | BI: 3 | C: N/A

### Translational Regulation

Polysomal profiling reveals striking ribosome association at ring stage (FC 55.5, top 4.4%, p=0.0036), suggesting this gene's mRNA is translationally engaged even when steady-state transcript levels are moderate.

- Polysomal and steady-state asexual stage transcriptomes (Bunnik et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: 2
  - This gene shows a dramatic increase in polysome-associated mRNA at ring stage compared to steady-state (FC 55.5, top 4.4% upregulated), with significant overall polysomal enrichment (p=0.0036).

### Transcription and mRNA Dynamics

Nascent RNA labelling indicates active transcription throughout the IDC with peak stability in early stages, supporting a model of constitutive transcription with post-transcriptional regulation.

- Pfal3D7 real-time transcription and decay () | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - In labelled transcripts, this gene shows modest transcriptional upregulation peaking at 47 hpi (FC 2.2, top 15.2%), while unlabelled mRNA shows stronger early accumulation at 8–12 hpi (FC 1.8, top 17.6–19.0%).

### Perturbation and Knockout Studies

This gene shows subtle but consistent downregulation in invasion pathway knockout lines and is largely unresponsive to chloroquine treatment or Sir2 knockouts.
