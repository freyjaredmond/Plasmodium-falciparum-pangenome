**Trophozoite-Peaking Gene with Strong Oocyst Enrichment and Antisense Regulation Across the <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i> Life Cycle**

This gene exhibits a sharp expression peak during the mid-to-late trophozoite stage of the intraerythrocytic development cycle (IDC), consistently observed across multiple strains, technologies, and experimental designs. Upregulation from ring to trophozoite is dramatic (FC 14.5, top 0.9%, p=1.4e-8 in Wichers et al.; FC 11.9, top 8.5%, p=1.2e-20 in Tang et al.), with peak expression at 28–32 hr post-invasion.

- **Oocyst-enriched across mosquito stages**: Expression is highest in midgut oocysts, with significant downregulation in salivary gland sporozoites (FC -9.8, p=3.2e-17) and near-silencing in mosquito sporozoites versus blood stages (FC -46.2, p=1.3e-3)
- **Antisense regulation**: Antisense transcripts peak at schizont stages and show significant changes across life cycle transitions, including upregulation in severe versus uncomplicated malaria (FC 2.1, p=6.3e-4)
- **Strain variation**: HB3 expresses this gene ~1.8-fold higher than 3D7 (p=6.4e-4) and ~2.5-fold higher than IT (p=3.5e-3)
- **Not responsive** to chloroquine, heat shock, or invasion pathway knockouts

The trophozoite peak and oocyst enrichment suggest involvement in metabolically active growth phases across both vertebrate and mosquito host stages.

## Key Experiments

### Intraerythrocytic Development Cycle Expression

This gene consistently peaks at the trophozoite-to-schizont transition (28–32 hr post-invasion) across multiple IDC time-course datasets, with fold changes up to 95-fold and exceptional statistical support in replicated studies.

- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2019) (Wichers et al. 2019) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - This gene is dramatically upregulated from late ring to mid-trophozoite (FC 14.5, top 0.9% upregulated, p=1.4e-8), the most extreme change in this dataset, with sustained high expression through late trophozoite and schizont stages, and significant downregulation from late schizont to merozoite (FC -2.3, top 16.1%, p=0.04).
- **Transcriptome of the asexual life stages (Tang et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - This gene is dramatically upregulated from ring to trophozoite (FC 11.9, top 8.5%, p=1.2e-20) and ring to schizont (FC 5.6, top 21.8%, p=4.3e-8), with antisense also showing strong schizont-to-trophozoite upregulation (FC 5.6, top 8.0%).
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by DAFT-Seq (3D7, HB3, IT, 2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - Across three strains, antisense transcripts peak at 24–32hr (FC 2.5–3.0, top 4.3–6.5%, p<2e-6), while sense transcripts show significant upregulation at 32–40hr (FC 2.6–2.9, top 13.7–25.3%, p<0.014); HB3 expresses this gene ~1.8-fold higher than 3D7 (p=6.4e-4).
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by UTR-Seq (2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - This gene is significantly upregulated at 32hr (FC 3.2, top 8.0%, p=2.1e-24) and 40hr (FC 2.4, top 9.2%, p=1.8e-11), confirming mid-to-late IDC peak expression.
- **Blood stage transcriptome (3D7) (Otto et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene peaks at 32 hr post-invasion during the trophozoite-to-schizont transition with strong upregulation from ring stage (FC 8.9 at 32hr, top 1.1% upregulated).
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2018) (Toenhake et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - Antisense transcripts are strongly upregulated at T25 and T30 (FC 8.0–8.1, top 2.9–4.2% upregulated), while sense transcripts show marked downregulation at T20 (FC -22.4, top 20.5%) followed by recovery at T35.
- **High resolution intraerythrocytic time course transcriptome by RNA-Seq (Kucharski and Tripathi et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene peaks sharply at 30hr (FC 15.6 vs 4hr, top 8.3% upregulated) with sustained high expression through 34hr (FC 14.6, top 14.4%) before declining.
- **Erythrocytic expression time series (3D7, DD2, HB3) (Bozdech et al. and Llinas et al.) (array)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - Across all three strains (3D7, DD2, HB3), this gene shows consistent modest upregulation in mid-to-late IDC stages compared to early rings, with the most notable changes in HB3 (FC 2.1 at 24–30hr, top 16.6%).
- **Pfal3D7 real-time transcription and decay** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - Labelled (newly transcribed) mRNA peaks at 28–32 hpi (FC 3.0–3.1, top 11–14% upregulated) while unlabelled (stable) transcripts peak similarly, indicating both active transcription and mRNA stability contribute to the mid-IDC expression peak.
- **IDC in constant temperature and darkness (2020) (Subudhi et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - Under constant conditions, sense transcripts peak dramatically at 32hr (FC 95.2, top 19.4%) and 24hr (FC 69.1, top 19.0%), confirming robust circadian-independent IDC-driven expression.
- **Strand specific transcriptome of the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (Siegel et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene is progressively upregulated through the IDC, peaking at 40hr schizont stage (FC 5.6 vs 10hr, top 17.5% upregulated).
- **Life cycle expression data (3D7) (Le Roch et al.) (array)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene is most notably upregulated from early trophozoite to late trophozoite (FC 1.9, top 4.8% upregulated), consistent with peak trophozoite expression.

### Mosquito Stage and Oocyst Expression

This gene is highly enriched in midgut oocysts and sharply downregulated in salivary gland sporozoites, representing one of the most significant life-cycle transitions observed.

- **Ring, Oocyst and Sporozoite Transcriptomes (Zanghi et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - Antisense transcripts are strongly downregulated from oocyst to ring and sporozoite stages (FC -24.6 and -29.6, top 5.9% and 5.5% downregulated), while sense transcripts show highly significant differential expression between oocyst and sporozoite (p=2.6e-14).
- **Oocyst and salivary gland sporozoite transcriptome comparison in <i>P. falciparum</i> (Lindner et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 5
  - This gene is strongly downregulated in salivary gland sporozoites (FC -9.8, top 12.6% downregulated, p=3.2e-17 for sense).
- **Asexual blood stages, salivary gland sporozoite and midgut oocyst transcriptomes (Gomez-Diaz) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5
  - This gene is strongly upregulated in midgut oocysts compared to asexual blood stages (sense FC 4.3, top 15% upregulated, p=4.5e-7), with antisense transcripts showing more extreme fold changes.
- **Mosquito or cultured sporozoites and blood stage transcriptome (NF54) (Hoffmann et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: 2
  - This gene is significantly downregulated in mosquito sporozoites compared to blood stages (FC -46.2, top 23.0% downregulated, p=1.3e-3) but shows no significant change between blood stages and cultured sporozoites.
- **Transcriptomes of 7 sexual and asexual life stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene peaks sharply at the late trophozoite stage with dramatic upregulation from early trophozoite (FC 29.7, top 5.7% upregulated) and notable expression in ookinetes (FC 6.7 vs gametocyte V, top 14.3%).
- **Comparison of in vitro versus aseptic mosquito produced sporozoites by RNA-seq (Eappen et al. 2022) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 2 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows minimal differential expression between in vitro and mosquito-produced sporozoites (FC 2.2, top 33.9%), indicating comparable expression regardless of production method.

### Sexual Stage and Gametocyte Expression

This gene maintains relatively stable expression across gametocyte stages, with modest sex-specific differences in antisense transcripts and progressive downregulation during gametocyte maturation.

- **Gametocyte Transcriptomes (Lasonder et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows modest upregulation in female versus male gametocytes in antisense transcripts (FC 1.8, top 15.3%) but near-identical sense expression (FC 1.1, top 75.9%).
- **Gametocyte stages I-V transcriptomes (Young et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene maintains relatively stable expression across gametocyte development stages I-V, with the largest change being a modest upregulation at day 2 (FC 1.6, top 10.6% upregulated).
- **Gametocyte time course from commitment to maturity (van Biljon et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene is progressively downregulated during gametocyte maturation from commitment, reaching its lowest point at 8 days post-induction (FC -2.0, top 13.4% downregulated).

### Clinical and Field Isolate Expression

Antisense transcripts of this gene are significantly upregulated in severe malaria, while expression remains stable across different field isolate origins.

- **Transcriptome in severe vs uncomplicated malaria (Tonkin-Hill et al 2018) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 3
  - Antisense transcripts are significantly upregulated in severe versus uncomplicated malaria (FC 2.1, top 12.6% upregulated, p=6.3e-4, effect size -1.76), while sense transcripts show a similar trend without statistical significance.
- **Dual transcriptomes of malaria-infected Gambian children (RNA-Seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 1
  - This gene shows no statistically significant differential expression between cerebral malaria, hyperlactatemia, and uncomplicated malaria clinical phenotypes across all comparisons (all p>0.05), though antisense transcripts show modest downregulation in uncomplicated versus hyperlactatemia cases (FC -2.4, top 0.9% downregulated, p=0.027).

### Epigenetic and Transcriptional Regulation

This gene shows evidence of regulation by bromodomain protein PfBDP1 and modest Sir2-dependent effects during the IDC.

- **Transcriptome of Bromodomain protein conditional knockouts over the IDC (Josling et al. 2015) (array)** | BI: 5 | C: N/A
  - This gene is modestly downregulated upon PfBDP1 knockdown (FC -1.1, top 8.3% downregulated), suggesting it may be among the genes positively regulated by this bromodomain protein.
- **Two Sir2 KO lines expression profiling (array)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows subtle but consistent upregulation in Sir2 knockout lines versus wild-type at ring and trophozoite stages, with modest downregulation at schizont stage in both sir2a and sir2b knockouts.

### Invasion Pathway and Cytoadherence

Expression correlates with cytoadherence receptor preference but is unaffected by invasion pathway knockouts.

- **Transcriptome of sequestration phenotypes (Kamaliddin et al) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene is moderately downregulated in CD36-binding parasites compared to HBEC-5i-binding (FC -4.4, top 13.5%) and ICAM1-binding phenotypes (FC -2.3, top 25.3%).
- **Invasion pathway knockouts (Stubbs et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows minimal expression changes across all invasion pathway knockout comparisons (EBA140, EBA175, PfRh2b, SIR2) relative to wild-type in both 3D7 and W2mef backgrounds.

### Drug Response

- **Three Isogenic Lines w/ CQ Treatment: expression profiles (Jiang et al.) (array)** | BI: 3 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows no meaningful expression changes in response to chloroquine treatment across all three isogenic <i>P. falciparum</i> PfCRT mutant lines (FC 1.0–1.1).

### Translational Regulation

- **Polysomal and steady-state asexual stage transcriptomes (Bunnik et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: 0
  - This gene shows striking translational enrichment at ring stage (polysomal vs steady-state FC 16.8, top 23.3% upregulated) but depletion at schizont and trophozoite stages, suggesting stage-specific translational regulation.

### Other

- **Strand specific transcriptomes of 4 life cycle stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 2 | C: N/A
  - This gene shows relatively stable expression across trophozoite, schizont, and gametocyte stages in both sense and antisense transcripts (FC 1.1–2.1), with minimal variation.
- **Heat shock response in sensitive mutants (LRR5, DHC) (Zhang et al. 2021) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: 0

- **Sexually vs asexually committed schizont transcriptional profiles (Pelle et al.) (array)** | BI: 0 | C: N/A

- **NSR-seq Transcript Profiling of malaria-infected pregnant women and children (Vignali et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 1 | C: N/A

- **Trophozoite and Schizont transcriptomes of PfBDP1HA (Josling et al 2015) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 0 | C: 1
