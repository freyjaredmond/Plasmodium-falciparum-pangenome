**Trophozoite-peaking gene with sporozoite enrichment and clinical malaria association**

This gene in <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i> shows a robust and conserved expression peak during the trophozoite stage of the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (IDC), with significant upregulation from rings to mid-trophozoite confirmed across multiple datasets and strains.

- **IDC trophozoite peak**: Expression rises 2.9–15.4-fold from early rings to ~16–28 hpi across multiple experiments, peaking at mid-trophozoite before declining sharply in schizonts. This is highly significant in UTR-Seq data (FC 2.9 at 16hr, p=3.2e-30) and DAFT-Seq (FC 3.6 at 24hr, p=1.1e-5).
- **Sporozoite enrichment**: Significantly upregulated 2.8-fold in salivary gland sporozoites versus oocysts (p=2.0e-11) and versus rings (p=9.9e-6), suggesting a role in sporozoite maturation.
- **Clinical malaria**: Upregulated 14-fold in cerebral versus uncomplicated malaria (top 1.2%, p=0.048) with strong sex-dependent expression (p=1.2e-6).
- **Gametocyte commitment**: Transiently upregulated during early gametocytogenesis (2.2-fold at day 4 post-induction).

The trophozoite-stage peak and sporozoite enrichment suggest involvement in active growth or host-cell remodelling processes.

## Key Experiments

### Intraerythrocytic Developmental Cycle Expression

Expression consistently peaks at mid-trophozoite stage (16–30 hpi) across strains and technologies, with significant upregulation from rings and sharp decline in schizonts.

- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by UTR-Seq (2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5 | Expression peaks at 16hr (2.9-fold over 0hr, top 6.1% upregulated, p=3.2e-30) with highly significant upregulation sustained through 24hr (FC 2.7, p=6.2e-24), followed by gradual decline, and significant downregulation from 24hr to 48hr (FC -1.9, p=4.6e-7), providing exceptionally well-powered confirmation of trophozoite-stage peak expression.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome by DAFT-Seq (3D7, HB3, IT, 2020) (Chappell et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 4 | Across three strains, sense expression peaks at 24hr (3.6-fold, top 12.2%, p=1.1e-5) with significant upregulation at 16–32hr and a significant 3.9-fold decline from 24hr to 48hr (top 9.5% downregulated, p=1.3e-5); antisense peaks at 40hr (2.9-fold, top 2.9%, p=2.7e-5), with no significant inter-strain differences.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2019) (Wichers et al. 2019) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 4 | Expression increases significantly from early ring to late ring (FC 3.6, top 15.4%, p=8.7e-5) and peaks at mid-trophozoite (116 TPM), then significantly declines from mid to late trophozoite (p=2.7e-5), with no significant changes during schizogony, confirming peak expression during the trophozoite stage.
- **Intraerythrocytic development cycle transcriptome (2018) (Toenhake et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Sense expression peaks strongly at T25 (188 TPM, 15.4-fold over T05, top 6% upregulated) and T30 (14.4-fold, top 7.8%), with antisense peaking at T40 (7.9-fold, top 12.9%), indicating robust trophozoite-to-schizont stage induction across both transcript orientations.
- **Blood stage transcriptome (3D7) (Otto et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Expression peaks at 24 hpi with a 4.2-fold increase over 0hr (top 8.4% upregulated), rising from early rings through mid-trophozoite before declining at later schizont stages, consistent with a trophozoite-stage expression peak during the IDC.
- **Erythrocytic expression time series (3D7, DD2, HB3) (Bozdech et al. and Llinas et al.) (array)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Across all three strains, expression peaks during mid-trophozoite stages (17–23 hpi) with the strongest induction in HB3 (2.9-fold, top 3.8% upregulated from 1–8hr to 17–23hr), while declining in late schizont stages, indicating a conserved trophozoite-stage expression peak.
- **Strand specific transcriptome of the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (Siegel et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Expression peaks at 20 hpi with a 4.3-fold increase over 10hr (top 8.2% upregulated), remains elevated at 30hr (4.4-fold), then sharply declines 4.0-fold from 30hr to 40hr (top 32.6% downregulated), confirming a trophozoite-stage expression peak.
- **High resolution intraerythrocytic time course transcriptome by RNA-Seq (Kucharski and Tripathi et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression peaks at ~28–30 hpi (mid-trophozoite) with a 7.1-fold increase over 4hr rings (top 33.3% upregulated) and 10.5-fold decline from 28hr to 52hr (top 20.9% downregulated), confirming a trophozoite-stage expression peak with sharp decline in schizonts.
- **IDC in constant temperature and darkness (2020) (Subudhi et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Under constant conditions, sense expression peaks at 16–24 hpi (~9.3-fold over 0hr, top 35% upregulated) before declining, while antisense shows strongest induction at 16hr (7.3-fold, top 16.8% upregulated), consistent with a circadian-independent trophozoite peak.
- **Pfal3D7 real-time transcription and decay ()** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Newly transcribed (labelled) mRNA peaks at 20 hpi (2.3-fold over 0hpi, top 21.1% upregulated) then declines through late stages, while unlabelled (stable) mRNA shows modest consistent upregulation (~1.5-fold) across all timepoints, suggesting active transcription peaks during trophozoite stage with relatively stable mRNA.
- **Transcriptome of the asexual life stages (Tang et al. 2020) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 4 | Expression peaks significantly at trophozoite stage (43.7 TPM, 4.2-fold over ring, top 36.6%, p=8.2e-5) with 4.7-fold increase from schizont to trophozoite (top 11.6%), and significant upregulation in sporozoite vs trophozoite comparisons (p=2.7e-5, effect size 1.50), while antisense shows 2.6-fold trophozoite enrichment over schizont.

### Sporozoite and Mosquito Stage Expression

This gene is significantly enriched in sporozoites relative to oocysts and blood stages, suggesting functional relevance during sporozoite maturation.

- **Oocyst and salivary gland sporozoite transcriptome comparison in P. falciparum (Lindner et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 5 | Expression is significantly upregulated 2.8-fold in salivary gland sporozoites versus oocysts (top 13.2% upregulated, p=2.0e-11, effect size 2.36), with consistent upregulation in both sense and antisense transcripts, indicating increased expression as sporozoites mature and gain infectivity.
- **Ring, Oocyst and Sporozoite Transcriptomes (Zanghi et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: 5 | Expression is significantly upregulated in sporozoites versus oocysts (2.8-fold, top 16.3%, p=1.3e-11, effect size 2.84) and versus rings (2.8-fold, top 16.4%, p=9.9e-6), with antisense also showing upregulation in sporozoites, confirming sporozoite-enriched expression.
- **Asexual blood stages, salivary gland sporozoite and midgut oocyst transcriptomes (Gomez-Diaz) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | This gene shows modest upregulation in mosquito stages versus asexual blood stages, with antisense transcripts ranking in the top 1.6–4.5% of upregulated genes in oocyst and sporozoite comparisons, though none reach statistical significance.
- **Comparison of in vitro versus aseptic mosquito produced sporozoites by RNA-seq (Eappen et al. 2022) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | The sense transcript is 8.6-fold downregulated in mosquito versus in vitro sporozoites (top 21% downregulated), while the antisense transcript is 8-fold upregulated (top 46.5%), suggesting divergent strand-specific regulation between production methods.

### Clinical and Field Isolate Expression

Significantly associated with malaria disease severity and patient sex in Gambian clinical isolates, with higher expression in field isolates than laboratory strains.

- **Dual transcriptomes of malaria-infected Gambian children (RNA-Seq)** | BI: 5 | C: 5 | This gene is significantly upregulated in uncomplicated malaria versus severe malaria phenotypes, with a 14-fold increase in cerebral malaria vs uncomplicated (top 1.2% upregulated, p=0.048), and highly significant sex-based differential expression (Female vs Male p=1.2e-6, top 3.7% significant genes), with antisense transcripts also showing consistent upregulation patterns across severity comparisons.
- **NSR-seq Transcript Profiling of malaria-infected pregnant women and children (Vignali et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | Expression is 2.6–3.0-fold lower in the 3D7 reference strain compared to field isolates from pregnant women and children (PLO1, PLO2), with modest variation between the two field isolate pools, suggesting moderately higher expression in clinical isolates.

### Sexual Stage and Gametocyte Expression

Transient upregulation during gametocyte commitment, with stable expression across gametocyte maturation stages.

- **Gametocyte time course from commitment to maturity (van Biljon et al.) (array)** | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Expression increases from pre-induction baseline during early gametocytogenesis, peaking at 4 days post-induction (2.2-fold, top 21% upregulated) before declining, with the strongest change being 1.7-fold downregulation from 2 days pre-induction to 0 days (top 9.4% downregulated), suggesting transient upregulation during commitment.
- **Gametocyte stages I-V transcriptomes (Young et al.) (array)** | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | Expression remains relatively stable across gametocyte stages I–V with only minor fluctuations (maximum 1.2-fold changes), indicating this gene is not developmentally regulated during gametocyte maturation.
- **Sexually vs asexually committed schizont transcriptional profiles (Pelle et al.) (array)** | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | Expression is slightly lower in sexually committed schizonts compared to asexual schizonts (1.2-fold downregulated, top 37.8% downregulated), indicating no substantial differential regulation during sexual commitment.
- **Transcriptomes of 7 sexual and asexual life stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression is highest in ookinetes (63.2 TPM) and gametocyte V (30.6 TPM), with a sharp 7.9-fold decline from late trophozoite to schizont (top 10.1% downregulated) and relatively stable expression across ring and trophozoite stages, indicating stage-specific regulation with a notable schizont drop.
- **Strand specific transcriptomes of 4 life cycle stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | Expression is low across all stages at the sense level (0.8–1.6 TPM, near floor), while antisense shows moderate expression with 3.0-fold downregulation from trophozoite to schizont (top 38.2% downregulated) and modest increases in gametocyte stages, suggesting primarily antisense-mediated regulation.

### Life Cycle and Multi-Stage Profiling

Broad life cycle profiling confirms trophozoite-enriched expression with constitutive baseline across stages.

- **Life cycle expression data (3D7) (Le Roch et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression is relatively stable across the IDC with a modest increase from early ring to late ring (1.3-fold, top 14.5% upregulated) and slight downregulation from late trophozoite to early schizogeny (1.4-fold down, top 20.6%), consistent with constitutive expression with minor trophozoite-stage enrichment.

### Drug Treatment and Perturbation Studies

No significant transcriptional response to chloroquine treatment, invasion pathway knockouts, Sir2 disruption, or PfBDP1 knockdown.

- **Invasion pathway knockouts (Stubbs et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression shows minimal changes across all invasion pathway knockout lines (EBA140, EBA175, PfRh2b, Sir2) compared to wild-type, with fold changes ≤1.1 across all comparisons, indicating this gene is not affected by disruption of these invasion ligands or epigenetic regulators.
- **Three Isogenic Lines w/ CQ Treatment: expression profiles (Jiang et al.) (array)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Chloroquine treatment produces minimal expression changes across all three isogenic PfCRT lines (maximum 1.1-fold), indicating this gene is not responsive to chloroquine exposure regardless of resistance genotype.
- **Two Sir2 KO lines expression profiling (array)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Both Sir2a and Sir2b knockouts show minimal expression changes versus wild-type across all three stages (maximum 1.0-fold), suggesting this gene is largely unaffected by Sir2 disruption.
- **Heat shock response in sensitive mutants (LRR5, DHC) (Zhang et al. 2021) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | This gene shows no significant differential expression between wild-type and either ΔDHC or ΔLRR5 heat-shock-sensitive mutants (all p>0.5), with minimal fold changes across all comparisons, indicating it is not involved in the heat shock sensitivity phenotype.

### Cytoadherence and Sequestration

Modest variation across binding phenotypes without dramatic phenotype-specific regulation.

- **Transcriptome of sequestration phenotypes (Kamaliddin et al) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression varies modestly across binding phenotypes with 2.5-fold downregulation in CD36-binding versus HBEC-5i-binding parasites (top 37.8% downregulated) and antisense showing consistent low-level differences, but no dramatic phenotype-specific regulation.

### Trophozoite vs Schizont Comparisons

Trophozoite enrichment confirmed in PfBDP1HA-tagged parasites.

- **Trophozoite and Schizont transcriptomes of PfBDP1HA (Josling et al 2015) (RNA-seq)** | BI: 3 | C: 1 | Expression is 3.0-fold higher in trophozoites than schizonts (top 27.4% upregulated, p=0.011, effect size 1.67), with antisense also showing 2.2-fold trophozoite enrichment (top 25.4%), consistent with trophozoite-stage predominant expression in PfBDP1HA parasites.
