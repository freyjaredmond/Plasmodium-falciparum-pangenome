**Ring-Stage-Enriched Blood Stage Gene with Translational Repression and Silencing in Mosquito Stages**

## Summary

This <i>Plasmodium falciparum</i> gene is predominantly expressed during the early ring stage of the intraerythrocytic developmental cycle (IDC), with consistent downregulation through trophozoite and schizont stages across multiple strains and experimental platforms. Expression peaks at ~200–470 TPM in early rings and declines 2- to 14-fold by mid-late IDC stages. Key findings include:

- **Strong ring-stage enrichment**: Significant downregulation from ring to trophozoite (FC -3.2, p=6.6e-12) and schizont stages, replicated across 3D7, HB3, IT, and DD2 strains, with IT showing significantly higher expression than other strains (FC 2.2 vs HB3, p=1.8e-4)
- **Translational repression**: Dramatic polysomal depletion at trophozoite stage (FC -16.8, top 4.6% downregulated), suggesting post-transcriptional regulation
- **Mosquito stage silencing**: Strongly downregulated in sporozoites versus blood stages (FC -48.8, p=1.4e-8)
- **Antisense regulation**: Notable antisense upregulation at schizont stage (FC 19.1, top 1.1%) may mediate sense-strand repression

These patterns suggest a role in early ring-stage biology, possibly related to erythrocyte remodelling or early invasion-related processes, with multi-layered transcriptional and translational control.

## Key Experiments

### Intraerythrocytic Developmental Cycle Expression

This gene consistently peaks in early rings and is progressively downregulated through the IDC, with multi-strain replication confirming ring-stage enrichment and strain-dependent expression levels (IT > 3D7 > HB3).

### Transcriptional and Post-Transcriptional Regulation

Active transcription declines sharply during mid-IDC, and the gene shows dramatic polysomal depletion at trophozoite/schizont stages, indicating dual transcriptional and translational control.

- Pfal3D7 real-time transcription and decay () | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Newly transcribed (labelled) mRNA shows progressive decline from 0hpi through the IDC, with strongest suppression at 47hpi (FC -2.9, top 6% downregulated), while unlabelled (pre-existing) mRNA declines more modestly (FC -1.4 to -1.9), suggesting both reduced transcription and moderate mRNA stability.
- Polysomal and steady-state asexual stage transcriptomes (Bunnik et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: 2 | This gene shows a dramatic reduction in polysomal versus steady-state mRNA at the trophozoite stage (FC -16.8, top 4.6% downregulated) and schizont stage (FC -6.9, top 16% downregulated), with overall significant polysomal depletion (p=0.002, effect size 2.2), indicating strong translational repression during mid-to-late IDC stages.

### Chromatin and Epigenetic Regulation

This gene may be partially dependent on the bromodomain protein PfBDP1 for expression, though absolute changes are modest.

- Transcriptome of Bromodomain protein conditional knockouts over the IDC (Josling et al. 2015) (array) | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | PfBDP1 knockdown (Shld1 removal) results in modest downregulation of this gene (FC -1.1, top 8.3% downregulated), placing it among the more strongly affected genes in this experiment.
- Two Sir2 KO lines expression profiling (array) | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Sir2a and Sir2b knockouts show only minor expression changes at this gene across ring, trophozoite, and schizont stages (FC -1.0 to 1.0), with the most notable being modest downregulation at the ring stage in Sir2a KO (FC -1.1, top 11% downregulated).

### Life Cycle and Mosquito Stage Expression

This gene is strongly silenced in mosquito stages relative to blood stages, with progressive downregulation from oocyst to salivary gland sporozoite.

- Asexual blood stages, salivary gland sporozoite and midgut oocyst transcriptomes (Gomez-Diaz) (RNA-seq) | BI: 5 | C: 1 | This gene is modestly downregulated in salivary gland sporozoites versus blood stages (FC -4.4, top 19% downregulated, p=0.016) with low expression across mosquito stages.
- Life cycle expression data (3D7) (Le Roch et al.) (array) | BI: 5 | C: DE not available | Across nine life cycle stages, this gene shows peak expression at late ring (log2 5.44) with significant downregulation into early trophozoite (FC -1.5, top 6.4% downregulated) and subsequent modest recovery through schizogeny.
- Ring, Oocyst and Sporozoite Transcriptomes (Zanghi et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 4 | C: 5 | This gene is significantly upregulated in rings versus oocysts on the sense strand (FC 1.4, p=1.6e-6) and significantly downregulated in sporozoites versus rings (FC -3.4, p=6.3e-9, top 15%), confirming ring-stage enrichment and progressive silencing across mosquito stages.
- Mosquito or cultured sporozoites and blood stage transcriptome (NF54) (Hoffmann et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 3 | C: 5 | This gene is strongly downregulated in mosquito sporozoites versus blood stages (FC -48.8, top 22% downregulated, p=1.4e-8), with intermediate expression in cultured sporozoites.
- Oocyst and salivary gland sporozoite transcriptome comparison in <i>P. falciparum</i> (Lindner et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 3 | C: 5 | This gene is significantly downregulated in salivary gland sporozoites compared to oocysts on the sense strand (FC -5.7, top 22% downregulated, p=4.5e-9), indicating transcriptional silencing during the oocyst-to-sporozoite transition.
- Strand specific transcriptomes of 4 life cycle stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | Sense strand expression is very low across all four stages (0–3.4 TPM) with notable antisense upregulation from trophozoite to schizont (FC 9.8, top 25% upregulated).

### Sexual Stage and Gametocyte Expression

This gene shows declining expression during gametocyte development, consistent with its asexual blood-stage enrichment, with modest male-biased expression.

- Gametocyte stages I-V transcriptomes (Young et al.) (array) | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | This gene is moderately expressed in early gametocytes (day 1, log2 5.08, top 21% expression) and shows modest downregulation through gametocyte maturation, most pronounced at day 6 and day 12 (FC -1.4 to -1.6, top 26–28% downregulated).
- Gametocyte time course from commitment to maturity (van Biljon et al.) (array) | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | Following gametocyte induction, this gene is progressively downregulated relative to pre-induction, peaking at day 6 post-induction (FC -2.1, top 20% downregulated), consistent with asexual-stage-enriched expression.
- Gametocyte Transcriptomes (Lasonder et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | This gene is moderately downregulated in females compared to males on the sense strand (FC -3.3, top 38% downregulated) with low overall gametocyte expression (~20 TPM male, ~6 TPM female).
- Transcriptomes of 7 sexual and asexual life stages (Lopez-Barragan et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 2 | C: DE not available | This gene peaks in rings (117 TPM) and declines through trophozoite and schizont stages (FC -2.1 early-to-late trophozoite), with further reduction in gametocyte stages and ookinete (14–18 TPM).

### Clinical and Field Isolate Expression

This gene shows an unexpected sex-associated expression difference in Gambian clinical isolates, though no association with disease severity.

- Dual transcriptomes of malaria-infected Gambian children (RNA-Seq) | BI: 4 | C: 4 | This gene shows no significant differential expression between cerebral malaria, hyperlactatemia, and uncomplicated malaria disease states, but is significantly associated with host sex (p=3.7e-5 sense, p=1.7e-4 antisense), with lower expression in males (effect size ~-0.8).
- NSR-seq Transcript Profiling of malaria-infected pregnant women and children (Vignali et al.) (RNA-seq) | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | Expression is relatively consistent between 3D7 and field isolates from pregnant women and children (10–15 TPM), with modest downregulation in PLO2 versus PLO1 (FC -1.4, top 14% downregulated).

### Sequestration and Cytoadherence Phenotypes

This gene is upregulated in CD36-binding parasites compared to ICAM1- and HBEC-5i-binding phenotypes, suggesting a potential link to the CD36-mediated sequestration phenotype.

- Transcriptome of sequestration phenotypes (Kamaliddin et al) (RNA-seq) | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | This gene is upregulated in CD36-binding parasites versus both ICAM1-binding (FC 3.1, top 11% upregulated) and HBEC-5i-binding phenotypes (FC 2.0, top 12% upregulated).

### Invasion Pathway and Drug Response

This gene is not substantially affected by invasion ligand knockouts or chloroquine treatment.

- Invasion pathway knockouts (Stubbs et al.) (array) | BI: 4 | C: DE not available | This gene shows minimal expression changes across invasion pathway knockout mutants (EBA140, EBA175, PfRh2b, Sir2) in both 3D7 and W2mef backgrounds (FC -1.2 to 1.4).
- Three Isogenic Lines w/ CQ Treatment: expression profiles (Jiang et al.) (array) | BI: 3 | C: DE not available | Chloroquine treatment causes no meaningful expression change in any of the three isogenic PfCRT lines (FC -1.0 to 1.1).
